# מעטפ״ה — five-part production methodology
From reference screenshot: **מנגנון, עריכה וצילום, טקסט, פסקול, הוק**. Key principle: **הרעיון: החלק היחיד שמשתנה** — preserve recurring identity but vary ONE creative element per episode.

## מ — מנגנון
Define business protagonist, stakes, two real costs, curiosity gap, operational insight, reveal, test, earned payoff and next episode loop. Choose ONE mechanic: timer, audience request, blind test, failed attempt, manual-vs-AI, duplicate lead. Record unanswered viewer question at 3s, 15s, 35s, 55s. No fake tension.
## ע — עריכה וצילום
A-CAM presenter, SCREEN actual app, MOTION minimal animated explanations. Specify shot, framing, source, cut motivation, on-screen text, cursor zoom, safe zones, transition. 9:16 1080x1920 30fps; legible Hebrew RTL. Deliver shotlist and EDL with frame ranges.
## ט — טקסט
Separate spoken VO, on-screen text and platform caption. Natural Hebrew, concrete nouns and active verbs. No jargon before pain. Verify all claims; script 13 beats and subtitles. Short mobile headlines.
## פ — פסקול
Clear VO first, licensed music/SFX, subtle UI sounds, silence at twist, one reveal hit and sonic logo. Timecode cues, duck under voice, check peaks.
## ה — הוק
First 0–3 seconds: real result, surprising test, owner pain or contradiction. Generate 12 alternatives and score audience fit, curiosity, specificity, proof and visual strength. No greeting or branding bumper.

For each axis output: choice, why, timestamp, asset, implementation, risk, metric and repair. Score 0–5; revise below 3. Mark exactly one `NOVEL_ELEMENT` per episode.
